How these are measured

Invoices, lines and value count item invoices each user posted. Invoices/day divides by the days the user posted anything. Delay is vendor invoice date to posting date. Backdated means the posting date is more than the late threshold before the day it was actually posted. Negative lines on invoices and credit memo share point to the document-type mistakes the team is working to remove.
