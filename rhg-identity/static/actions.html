SLA matrix

Days from the day an item is found to its deadline, by the department that owns it and its severity. The registers of Finance and Purchasing date every new item with it; "Apply to open items" re-dates the open items nobody re-dated by hand.

Daily email

Who owns what

The role that owns each type of item in the registers. Choose a role of Administration (its accounts own the items of the companies and restaurants their access covers) or a role of Email recipients; leave it on the register's rule otherwise. New items take it within 5 minutes; "Apply the matrices to open items" hands the open ones over (not those handed over by hand).

Roles of Administration

The roles created in Administration → Roles & permissions and their accounts (Administration → Accounts), with the access of each. A role chosen above gives its items to these people; to add someone, give them the role in Administration.

The department of each role decides the row of the SLA matrix its items follow and the head of department they escalate to. It starts from the role's department in Administration; change it here when it differs.

Roles of Email recipients

For every company (and restaurant), the people in each role of Email recipients. The same directory as Settings → Email recipients by company; it also routes the reports. An owner is a pulse account when its email matches one.
