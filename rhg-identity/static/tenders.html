Keywords and categories

One category per line: name | keywords, comma separated | cpv=CPV code prefixes; add | related for a category of less weight (its tenders are listed, but left out of the open value). A tender matches when its object has one of the words (accents and capitals set aside, at the start of a word) or one of its CPV codes starts with a prefix. A tender matched by mistake: take the word that matched (shown on the tender) out of its line.
