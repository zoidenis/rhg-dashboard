Connection to the Tax Administration

Every morning the tower reads the e-invoices of the last days with the fiscalization certificate (reading only, nothing is registered), reconciles them and emails the result. The certificate and its password stay on the server and are never entered here.

Recipe coverage & integrity

Which items need a recipe, which have a valid one, and which sell without one. Business Central is read only.

to

Coverage by location

Items sold at each location in the period and the state of their recipe. Click a number to see the items. Coverage = valid / items that require a recipe.

Missing recipes

Items that need a recipe and have no Assembly BOM, sold or not, most sold first. In Business Central they sell with no ingredient consumption.

Incomplete and inactive recipes

A BOM exists but a component is missing or blocked, a quantity is zero, a unit has no conversion or a sub-recipe is broken.

Pending review

Sold items whose need for a recipe cannot be confirmed. Most are set up as sold as purchased but have no unit cost, which usually means a combo or menu item without a recipe.
