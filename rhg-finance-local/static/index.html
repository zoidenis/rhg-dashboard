Which items need a recipe, which have a valid one, and which sell without one. Business Central is read only.

to

Coverage by location

Items sold at each location in the period and the state of their recipe. Click a number to see the items. Coverage = valid / items that require a recipe.

Missing recipes

Items that need a recipe and have no Assembly BOM, sold or not, most sold first. In Business Central they sell with no ingredient consumption.

Incomplete and inactive recipes

A BOM exists but a component is missing or blocked, a quantity is zero, a unit has no conversion or a sub-recipe is broken.

Pending review

Sold items whose need for a recipe cannot be confirmed. Most are set up as sold as purchased but have no unit cost, which usually means a combo or menu item without a recipe.

Receivables & payables email

A daily email with the exceptions of the Payables and Receivables control sections: overdue beyond the critical limit, supplier accounts in debit and concentration, with the ageing; every exception, document by document, comes as an attached PDF. It is sent through the mail server set up in Purchasing → Settings, so no password is entered here.

Off

Cost control weekly email

Every week, for the week before, a short report for the cost controller: highlights against the previous report (stock against the G/L, slow and dead stock, stock received and never used, days of cover, items below zero, recipe coverage, item master issues), an action list with priority, owner and value, and five lines each from Inventory, Movement & dead stock, Typology & turnover, Recipe integrity and Item master control. Every line behind it comes in an Excel file. Sent through the Purchasing mail server.

Off

Brand performance email

One email per brand, to the brand's own addresses with finance and operations in Cc. Weekly, for the week before: sales of every location (Simphony, excluding VAT) against the budget, the week before and the same weekdays last year; guests, checks and the average check; sales by day; the month to date with the costs posted so far. Monthly, for the month before, twice: provisional on the first working day and final on the day set below, once payroll and stock usage are posted: the P&L of the brand (sales, food & beverage, labour, prime cost, other costs, EBITDA) against budget and last year, the month and the year to date, every location. Brands and locations come from the registry in Location intelligence. Sent through the Purchasing mail server.
